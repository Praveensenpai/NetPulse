# NetPulse v1.4.6

**Status bar speed glyph, fixed for real this time.**

## What changed

- **No more stretched digits.** The glyph is drawn as a single centred run
  sized to fit the icon canvas, so it is never horizontally squeezed. The
  distortion came from forcing four characters into a square slot.
- **Readable at a glance.** Labels cap at three characters: `5.8M` shows as
  `5M`, and `988K` promotes to `1M` instead of being truncated to a number off
  by an order of magnitude.
- **Matches the clock.** Measured on device: the two-character glyph is 27 px
  tall against a 27 px clock, with the digits' natural proportions.

## Correction to v1.4.5

The v1.4.5 notes claimed the glyph measured 39x28 px. On device it measured
15x37 px: stacking the unit under the number overflowed the status-bar row.
v1.4.6 replaces that layout rather than patching it.
