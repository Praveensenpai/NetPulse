## 🛡️ NetPulse v1.3.2 — No More Frozen Apps

Stops a per-app total from getting stuck at `0 B` for the rest of the day.

### 🐛 Fixed

**A baseline could outlive its history.** v1.3.0 stores each app's cumulative byte count at midnight and reports `current - baseline`. Android's netstats trims old buckets, and a reboot resets the counters, so `current` can fall *below* the stored baseline. The old code clamped that negative to zero, which froze the app at `0 B` until the next midnight.

A regression is now detected explicitly (`sinceBaseline` returns null), the app is re-baselined on its current total, and reporting resumes on the next poll.

- Lint clean, `assembleDebug` and `assembleRelease` green.
- On device: live per-app rows, live rates during a 12 MB transfer, fresh minute log rows, no exceptions.
